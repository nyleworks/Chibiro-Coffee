Chibiro Stamp Card

Every visit earns a cat stamp. Collect all ten cats, and the next drink is on us.

How it works

  1. 1Get your free card right here. No sign-up, no app and no personal details.
  2. 2After you order, show your card's QR code at the counter. We stamp one cat per visit.
  3. 3Collect all ten cats, and your next drink is free.

Your member number arrives with your first stamp. A card expires if a month goes by with no new stamp.

    Chibiro Stamp Card

    Every visit earns a cat stamp. Your card starts with 3 cats on us, so a free drink is just 7 visits away.

    How it works

    1. 1Get your free card right here. It starts with 3 welcome stamps. No sign-up, no app and no personal details.
    2. 2After you order, show your card's QR code at the counter. We stamp one cat per visit.
    3. 3Collect all ten cats, and your next drink is free. Your next card starts with 1 bonus stamp.

    Your member number arrives with your first visit. A card expires if a month goes by with no new stamp.

    Photo Dump

    Snap your visit, post it on Instagram and tag Chibiro Coffee. We pick our 10 favourite shots and hang them up here.

    Please keep your stamp card's QR code out of the picture: anyone who scans it can use your card.
